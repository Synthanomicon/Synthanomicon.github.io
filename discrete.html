Welcome to Discrete Math

Discrete Math is the study of mathematical structures that are atomic units. This is different from the mathematics you may be familiar with from primary school which tends to focus on continuous mathematics. In the world of computers however discrete math is the basis for all of the technology you encounter in your day to day activities.

Here there are practice problem sets for each of the videos on the synthanomicon channel so you can apply what you learn and check your understanding. Be sure to give the problems a try before looking at the answers.
